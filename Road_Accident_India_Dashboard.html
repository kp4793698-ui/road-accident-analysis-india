Key Insights
  • Accidents peak at 2:00 and are lowest at 7:00. The busiest time period is Night (0-5).
  • Monday has the most accidents (2,966) and Wednesday the fewest (2,726); weekends hold 28.6% of accidents.
  • 44.9% of accidents are major or fatal; the most common severity is minor.
  • clear weather has the highest share of severe accidents (45.0%). This is an association, not a cause.
  • The most common recorded cause is distraction (20.1% of accidents).
  • Maharashtra has the most records, and Chandigarh is the top city.
  • Among road features, "traffic_density: medium" has the highest severe share (45.4%).
